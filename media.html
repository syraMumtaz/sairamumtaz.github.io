Short biography

Survivor, mother, IT Project Manager and author.

Saira Mumtaz is a survivor, mother, IT Project Manager and author of I Built the Door. Her work explores high-functioning trauma, cultural silence, single motherhood, ADHD in women, perimenopause and generational healing. She shares her story as @sairatalk on TikTok and Instagram.
