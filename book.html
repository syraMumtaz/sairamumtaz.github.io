The Book

A story about what happens when survival is no longer enough.

I Built the Door is the memoir no one was supposed to write. It follows a girl raised to disappear into service, a mother who chose the unknown for her children, and a woman who discovered that functioning was not the same as healing.

Saira Mumtaz writes about abuse, arranged marriage, family separation, single motherhood, high-functioning trauma, ADHD, perimenopause and the slow work of building a life that does not require self-erasure.

Inside the book

Three parts. One truth.

Part I

The Cost of Survival

The cost of survival and the woman who learned to look fine.

Part II

Understanding the Root

The roots: body, relationships, motherhood, money and the nervous system.

Part III

Becoming

Becoming: rest, desire, boundaries, safety, voice and visibility.

Who it is for

For people praised for strength while privately unraveling.

For survivors of families, relationships, cultures or systems that required silence. For working mothers, first-generation professionals and anyone learning that they do not have to prove their worth through exhaustion.
